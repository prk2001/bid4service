Casualty Insurance

Auto Insurance

Personal and commercial auto coverage tailored to your driving needs and budget.

  • Liability coverage for bodily injury and property damage
  • Collision coverage for accident damage
  • Comprehensive coverage for theft, weather, and vandalism
  • Uninsured/underinsured motorist protection
  • Multi-car and bundling discounts available

General Liability

Business protection from third-party claims that could threaten your livelihood.

  • Coverage for bodily injury to third parties
  • Property damage liability
  • Legal defense costs
  • Medical payments coverage
  • Personal and advertising injury protection

Umbrella Policies

Extra liability protection beyond your primary policies for serious claims.

  • Additional coverage above auto and home limits
  • Affordable way to add $1-5M in protection

  • Protects assets from major lawsuits

Required coverage for Georgia businesses with 3+ employees.

  • Medical expense coverage for work injuries
  • Disability income for injured workers
  • Death benefits for families
  • Protects your business from lawsuits

Property Insurance

Homeowners Insurance

Comprehensive protection for your home and everything in it.

  • Dwelling coverage for your home's structure
  • Personal property protection for belongings
  • Liability coverage for accidents on your property
  • Protection from fire, theft, and weather damage
  • Replacement cost vs. actual cash value options

Renters Insurance

Affordable protection for your belongings and peace of mind.

  • Personal property coverage for furniture, electronics, clothing
  • Liability coverage included
  • Additional living expenses if displaced
  • Often required by landlords
  • Typically costs less than $20/month

Condo/Co-op Insurance

Coverage designed specifically for unit owners.

  • Interior structure and improvements coverage
  • Personal property protection
  • Liability coverage for guests
  • Complements your HOA's master policy
  • Loss assessment coverage available

Commercial Property

Protect your business assets from unexpected loss.

  • Buildings and structures coverage
  • Equipment and inventory protection
  • Business interruption coverage
  • Accounts receivable protection
  • Valuable papers and records coverage

Life Insurance

Term Life Insurance

Affordable coverage for a set period when you need it most.

  • Coverage periods: 10, 20, or 30 years
  • Fixed premiums for the entire term
  • Ideal for income replacement during working years
  • Debt protection (mortgage, student loans)
  • Most affordable life insurance option

Whole Life Insurance

Permanent coverage that builds cash value over time.

  • Lifetime coverage—never expires
  • Fixed premiums that never increase
  • Guaranteed death benefit
  • Cash value grows tax-deferred
  • Can borrow against cash value if needed

Universal Life Insurance

Flexible coverage that adapts to your changing needs.

  • Adjustable premiums and death benefit
  • Cash value tied to market performance
  • More flexibility than whole life
  • Options for guaranteed or variable returns
  • Good for long-term wealth transfer planning

Not sure which type is right for you? We'll walk you through your options based on your goals, budget, and family situation. There's no pressure—just honest guidance to help you make the best decision.
