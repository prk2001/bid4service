Our Story

After years in the insurance industry, I founded Clearview Mutual to fix what's broken: brokers locked into single carriers, opaque pricing, and clients treated like policy numbers instead of people.

I saw too many families overpaying for coverage they didn't understand, and businesses getting stuck with one-size-fits-all policies that didn't fit their actual risks. There had to be a better way.

Clearview Mutual is that better way. As an independent broker, we're not beholden to any single insurance company. We work for you—comparing quotes from top-rated carriers, explaining your options in plain English, and fighting for you when claims arise.

Insurance should be simple, fair, and tailored to your life. That's what we deliver.

Patrick

Founder, Clearview Mutual
